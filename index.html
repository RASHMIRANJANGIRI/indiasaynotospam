We fight against spam because not every platform gives you full protection. Gmail has a built-in spam folder, and Truecaller helps identify unknown numbers, but there's no reliable way to detect spam on WhatsApp messages or across all platforms together. That's why we built SayNoToSpam — a single tool that helps you check and detect spam in Gmail, WhatsApp, SMS easily and safely.

With SayNoToSpam, we aim to reduce such losses by helping people identify and avoid digital scams before it's too late.

How we target spam

SayNoToSpam flags a message, number, or email as spam based on a combination of automated AI analysis and predefined rules. Users can request a review after the result is shown.
